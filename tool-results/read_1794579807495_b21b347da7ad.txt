     1→MASTER DEVELOPMENT PROMPT
     2→Physiotherapy Clinic Management System: GMRS
     3→1. Project Overview
     4→Build a modern, professional, full-stack Physiotherapy Clinic Management System designed to manage the daily operations and clinical workflows of a physiotherapy clinic.
     5→The application must manage the complete patient journey, starting from patient registration and appointment booking through clinical assessment, diagnosis, treatment planning, therapy sessions, progress reviews, billing, and eventual discharge.
     6→The system must also support staff management, clinic administration, insurance configuration, packages, reporting and operational dashboards.
     7→This is a real clinic management application, not a static UI prototype. All screens, forms, navigation, business rules, database relationships and backend operations must be implemented and connected.
     8→The application should be designed with future multi-clinic support in mind, even if the initial deployment serves one clinic.
     9→Primary objectives
    10→* Centralize patient records.
    11→* Manage appointments and therapist availability.
    12→* Maintain structured clinical documentation.
    13→* Track patient treatment journeys.
    14→* Manage therapy sessions and rehabilitation progress.
    15→* Support PAYG, package and private insurance billing.
    16→* Track clinic revenue and outstanding balances.
    17→* Monitor therapist performance and workload.
    18→* Provide management with operational and clinical reports.
    19→* Maintain secure, auditable patient and financial records.

    38→* TypeScript.
    39→* REST API architecture.
    40→* Modular monolith architecture.
    41→* Prisma ORM.
    42→* DTO-based validation.
    43→* class-validator and class-transformer.
    44→* Swagger / OpenAPI documentation.
    45→Database
    46→* PostgreSQL.
    47→Authentication
    48→* JWT access tokens.
    49→* Refresh tokens with rotation.
    50→* Role-based access control.
    51→* Permission-based authorization.
    52→* Secure password hashing.
    53→Architecture principles
    54→* Keep the frontend and backend as separate applications.
    55→* All business rules must be implemented and enforced in NestJS.
    56→* The frontend must communicate with the backend through documented REST APIs.
    57→* Do not connect the frontend directly to PostgreSQL.
    58→* Use Prisma for database access.
    59→* Use database transactions for operations involving multiple related records.
    60→* Use reusable frontend components.
    61→* Organize both applications by feature/module.
    62→* Keep business logic separate from controllers and UI components.
    63→* Use environment variables for application configuration.
    64→* Implement centralized error handling and consistent API responses.
    65→* Document APIs using Swagger.
    66→Do not make a final decision about file storage providers or deployment infrastructure yet. Keep those areas abstracted so they can be configured later.

   638→12. Therapy Session Management
   639→Therapy sessions represent the actual treatment delivered to the patient.
   640→They are separate from appointments.
   641→One appointment can generate one therapy session for the initial implementation.
   642→Session creation
   643→Therapy sessions should normally be created from attended appointments.
   644→Automatically populate:
   645→* Patient.
   646→* Treatment episode.
   647→* Appointment.
   648→* Assigned therapist.
   649→* Session date.
   650→Session documentation
   651→Capture:
   652→* Session date.
   653→* Pain level.
   654→* Blood pressure.
   655→* Other relevant vital signs.
   656→* Interventions delivered.
   657→* Treatment duration.
   658→* Patient response.
   659→* Clinical observations.
   660→* Additional notes.
   661→* Therapist signature.
   662→The therapist should document what actually happened, not simply copy the planned treatment interventions.
   663→Session statuses
   664→* Planned.
   665→* In Progress.
   666→* Completed.
   667→* Cancelled.
   668→* No-show.
   669→Allow the therapist to save an incomplete session as a draft or in-progress record.
   670→Only completed therapy sessions should count towards progress review thresholds and therapist completed-session statistics.
   671→Important distinction
   672→An appointment may be completed without a therapy session being completed.
   673→Examples:
   674→* Patient attended and received treatment: completed appointment and completed therapy session.
   675→* Patient did not attend: appointment marked no-show, no completed therapy session.
   676→* Patient attended but treatment was postponed: attended/no treatment, no completed therapy session.
   677→* Patient cancelled: cancelled appointment, no completed therapy session.

   679→13. Progress Review Management
   680→Progress reviews evaluate patient improvement and treatment effectiveness.
   681→The default review threshold is every 15 completed therapy sessions.
   682→A therapist can initiate an earlier review at any time.
   683→The session count must reset for the next review cycle after a completed formal review.
   684→Only completed therapy sessions count. Booked, cancelled, missed and incomplete sessions must not count.
   685→Progress review sections
   686→A. Previous clinical information
   687→Display:
   688→* Initial assessment.
   689→* Baseline measurements.
   690→* Diagnosis.
   691→* Treatment objectives.
   692→* Rehabilitation milestones.
   693→* Previous treatment plan.
   694→B. Updated clinical assessment
   695→Capture:
   696→* Current pain levels.
   697→* Current ROM.
   698→* Muscle strength.
   699→* Functional ability.
   700→* Vital signs where relevant.
   701→* New clinical findings.
   702→C. Baseline comparison
   703→Display original measurements beside current measurements.
   704→Examples:
   705→* Pain: 8/10 → 4/10.
   706→* Knee flexion: 90° → 115°.
   707→* Muscle strength: 2/5 → 4/5.
   708→* Walking difficulty: Severe → Mild.
   709→D. Objective and milestone evaluation
   710→Evaluate every objective and milestone.
   711→Statuses:
   712→* Achieved.
   713→* Partially Achieved.
   714→* Not Achieved.
   715→* No Longer Applicable.
   716→E. Therapist summary
   717→Capture:
   718→* Improvements.
   719→* Deterioration.
   720→* Patient response.
   721→* Clinical observations.
   722→* Recommendations.
   723→F. Review decision
   724→Allow the therapist to:
   725→* Continue existing plan.
   726→* Modify treatment interventions.
   727→* Modify objectives.
   728→* Adjust session frequency.
   729→* Revise diagnosis where clinically justified.
   730→* Refer for further investigation.
   731→* Discharge the patient.
   732→When the treatment plan changes, create a new version.
   733→Do not overwrite historical plans or reviews.

   735→14. Discharge Management
   736→Discharge must be therapist-led.
   737→The system may identify patients who appear ready for discharge, but must not automatically discharge them.
   738→Discharge reasons
   739→* Treatment objectives achieved.
   740→* Significant improvement.
   741→* Patient requested to stop.
   742→* Referral to another healthcare provider.
   743→* Treatment no longer clinically indicated.
   744→* Patient unable or unwilling to continue.

  1190→25. Important Business Rules
  1191→The following rules are mandatory.
  1192→1. One patient can have multiple treatment episodes.
  1193→2. Each treatment episode maintains its own clinical records.
  1194→3. Appointments and therapy sessions are separate entities.
  1195→4. A completed appointment does not automatically mean a completed therapy session.
  1196→5. Only completed therapy sessions count towards progress review thresholds.
  1197→6. Cancelled appointments and no-shows must not count.
  1198→7. Attended appointments without treatment must not count.
  1199→8. Progress reviews default to every 15 completed sessions.
  1200→9. The review counter resets after each completed formal review.
  1201→10. Early progress reviews must be supported.
  1202→11. Treatment plans must have version history.
  1203→12. Historical diagnoses must be retained when diagnoses change.
  1204→13. Completed clinical records must not be silently overwritten.
  1205→14. Discharge is therapist-led.
  1206→15. Discharge closes a treatment episode, not the patient.
  1207→16. Outstanding payments must not block clinical discharge.
  1208→17. Package sessions are deducted only after qualifying completed therapy sessions.
  1209→18. Package deductions must be reversible through auditable transactions.
  1210→19. Insurance rates must be configurable by provider.
  1211→20. Historical invoices must preserve the rates originally applied.
  1212→21. Therapist remuneration rules must be configurable.
  1213→22. Reports must distinguish unique patients from total completed sessions.
  1214→23. Every protected backend operation must validate the user's permissions and clinic access.
  1215→24. Financial transactions must be protected against duplicate processing.
  1216→25. Clinical and financial history must remain auditable.
