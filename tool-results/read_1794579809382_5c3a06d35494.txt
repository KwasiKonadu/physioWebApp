     1→     1→MASTER DEVELOPMENT PROMPT
     2→     2→Physiotherapy Clinic Management System: GMRS
     3→     3→1. Project Overview
     4→     4→Build a modern, professional, full-stack Physiotherapy Clinic Management System designed to manage the daily operations and clinical workflows of a physiotherapy clinic.
     5→     5→The application must manage the complete patient journey, starting from patient registration and appointment booking through clinical assessment, diagnosis, treatment planning, therapy sessions, progress reviews, billing, and eventual discharge.
     6→     6→The system must also support staff management, clinic administration, insurance configuration, packages, reporting and operational dashboards.
     7→     7→This is a real clinic management application, not a static UI prototype. All screens, forms, navigation, business rules, database relationships and backend operations must be implemented and connected.
     8→     8→The application should be designed with future multi-clinic support in mind, even if the initial deployment serves one clinic.
     9→     9→Primary objectives
    10→    10→* Centralize patient records.
    11→    11→* Manage appointments and therapist availability.
    12→    12→* Maintain structured clinical documentation.
    13→    13→* Track patient treatment journeys.
    14→    14→* Manage therapy sessions and rehabilitation progress.
    15→    15→* Support PAYG, package and private insurance billing.
    16→    16→* Track clinic revenue and outstanding balances.
    17→    17→* Monitor therapist performance and workload.
    18→    18→* Provide management with operational and clinical reports.
    19→    19→* Maintain secure, auditable patient and financial records.

    37→    37→* NestJS.
    38→    38→* TypeScript.
    39→    39→* REST API architecture.
    40→    40→* Modular monolith architecture.
    41→    41→* Prisma ORM.

    45→    45→Database
    46→    46→* PostgreSQL.
    47→    47→Authentication
    48→    48→* JWT access tokens.

    50→    50→* Role-based access control.
    51→    51→* Permission-based authorization.
    52→    52→* Secure password hashing.
    53→    53→Architecture principles
    54→    54→* Keep the frontend and backend as separate applications.
    55→    55→* All business rules must be implemented and enforced in NestJS.
    56→    56→* The frontend must communicate with the backend through documented REST APIs.
    57→    57→* Do not connect the frontend directly to PostgreSQL.
    58→    58→* Use Prisma for database access.
    59→    59→* Use database transactions for operations involving multiple related records.
    60→    60→* Use reusable frontend components.
    61→    61→* Organize both applications by feature/module.
    62→    62→* Keep business logic separate from controllers and UI components.
    63→    63→* Use environment variables for application configuration.
    64→    64→* Implement centralized error handling and consistent API responses.
    65→    65→* Document APIs using Swagger.
    66→    66→Do not make a final decision about file storage providers or deployment infrastructure yet. Keep those areas abstracted so they can be configured later.

   638→   638→12. Therapy Session Management
   639→   639→Therapy sessions represent the actual treatment delivered to the patient.
   640→   640→They are separate from appointments.
   641→   641→One appointment can generate one therapy session for the initial implementation.
   642→   642→Session creation
   643→   643→Therapy sessions should normally be created from attended appointments.
   644→   644→Automatically populate:
   645→   645→* Patient.
   646→   646→* Treatment episode.
   647→   647→* Appointment.
   648→   648→* Assigned therapist.
   649→   649→* Session date.
   650→   650→Session documentation
   651→   651→Capture:
   652→   652→* Session date.
   653→   653→* Pain level.
   654→   654→* Blood pressure.
   655→   655→* Other relevant vital signs.
   656→   656→* Interventions delivered.
   657→   657→* Treatment duration.
   658→   658→* Patient response.
   659→   659→* Clinical observations.
   660→   660→* Additional notes.
   661→   661→* Therapist signature.
   662→   662→The therapist should document what actually happened, not simply copy the planned treatment interventions.
   663→   663→Session statuses
   664→   664→* Planned.
   665→   665→* In Progress.
   666→   666→* Completed.
   667→   667→* Cancelled.
   668→   668→* No-show.
   669→   669→Allow the therapist to save an incomplete session as a draft or in-progress record.
   670→   670→Only completed therapy sessions should count towards progress review thresholds and therapist completed-session statistics.
   671→   671→Important distinction
   672→   672→An appointment may be completed without a therapy session being completed.
   673→   673→Examples:
   674→   674→* Patient attended and received treatment: completed appointment and completed therapy session.
   675→   675→* Patient did not attend: appointment marked no-show, no completed therapy session.
   676→   676→* Patient attended but treatment was postponed: attended/no treatment, no completed therapy session.
   677→   677→* Patient cancelled: cancelled appointment, no completed therapy session.
   678→   678→
   679→   679→13. Progress Review Management
   680→   680→Progress reviews evaluate patient improvement and treatment effectiveness.
   681→   681→The default review threshold is every 15 completed therapy sessions.
   682→   682→A therapist can initiate an earlier review at any time.
   683→   683→The session count must reset for the next review cycle after a completed formal review.
   684→   684→Only completed therapy sessions count. Booked, cancelled, missed and incomplete sessions must not count.
   685→   685→Progress review sections
   686→   686→A. Previous clinical information
   687→   687→Display:
   688→   688→* Initial assessment.
   689→   689→* Baseline measurements.
   690→   690→* Diagnosis.
   691→   691→* Treatment objectives.
   692→   692→* Rehabilitation milestones.
   693→   693→* Previous treatment plan.
   694→   694→B. Updated clinical assessment
   695→   695→Capture:
   696→   696→* Current pain levels.
   697→   697→* Current ROM.
   698→   698→* Muscle strength.
   699→   699→* Functional ability.
   700→   700→* Vital signs where relevant.
   701→   701→* New clinical findings.
   702→   702→C. Baseline comparison
   703→   703→Display original measurements beside current measurements.
   704→   704→Examples:
   705→   705→* Pain: 8/10 → 4/10.
   706→   706→* Knee flexion: 90° → 115°.
   707→   707→* Muscle strength: 2/5 → 4/5.
   708→   708→* Walking difficulty: Severe → Mild.
   709→   709→D. Objective and milestone evaluation
   710→   710→Evaluate every objective and milestone.
   711→   711→Statuses:
   712→   712→* Achieved.
   713→   713→* Partially Achieved.
   714→   714→* Not Achieved.
   715→   715→* No Longer Applicable.
   716→   716→E. Therapist summary
   717→   717→Capture:
   718→   718→* Improvements.
   719→   719→* Deterioration.
   720→   720→* Patient response.
   721→   721→* Clinical observations.
   722→   722→* Recommendations.
   723→   723→F. Review decision
   724→   724→Allow the therapist to:
   725→   725→* Continue existing plan.
   726→   726→* Modify treatment interventions.
   727→   727→* Modify objectives.
   728→   728→* Adjust session frequency.
   729→   729→* Revise diagnosis where clinically justified.
   730→   730→* Refer for further investigation.
   731→   731→* Discharge the patient.
   732→   732→When the treatment plan changes, create a new version.
   733→   733→Do not overwrite historical plans or reviews.

   735→   735→14. Discharge Management
   736→   736→Discharge must be therapist-led.
   737→   737→The system may identify patients who appear ready for discharge, but must not automatically discharge them.
   738→   738→Discharge reasons
   739→   739→* Treatment objectives achieved.
   740→   740→* Significant improvement.
   741→   741→* Patient requested to stop.
   742→   742→* Referral to another healthcare provider.
   743→   743→* Treatment no longer clinically indicated.
   744→   744→* Patient unable or unwilling to continue.
   745→   745→* Other.
   746→   746→A patient request to stop treatment should initiate a clinical review rather than automatically closing the episode.
   747→   747→Patients who stop attending must not be classified as successfully recovered.
   748→   748→Discharge workflow
   749→   749→1. Therapist initiates discharge.
   750→   750→2. Complete final clinical assessment.
   751→   751→3. Compare baseline and final measurements.
   752→   752→4. Evaluate objectives.
   753→   753→5. Evaluate rehabilitation milestones.
   754→   754→6. Select discharge reason.
   755→   755→7. Prepare discharge summary.
   756→   756→8. Enter home exercise recommendations.
   757→   757→9. Add follow-up or referral arrangements.
   758→   758→10. Therapist signs off.
   759→   759→11. Close the treatment episode.
   760→   760→Discharge information
   761→   761→Capture:
   762→   762→* Patient.
   763→   763→* Treatment episode.
   764→   764→* Diagnosis.
   765→   765→* Assigned therapist.

   767→   767→* Discharge reason.
   768→   768→* Final clinical assessment.
   769→   769→* Baseline comparison.
   770→   770→* Objective outcomes.
   771→   771→* Remaining limitations.
   772→   772→* Discharge summary.
   773→   773→* Follow-up arrangements.
   774→   774→* Therapist signature.
   775→   775→Home exercise recommendations
   776→   776→Do not implement an exercise library or predefined exercise templates in the initial version.
   777→   777→Allow the therapist to enter recommendations manually.
   778→   778→Fields:
   779→   779→* Exercise or activity name.
   780→   780→* Instructions.
   781→   781→* Frequency.
   782→   782→* Duration.
   783→   783→* Repetitions where applicable.
   784→   784→* Precautions.
   785→   785→* Notes.
   786→   786→Follow-up

   790→   790→* Telephone follow-up.
   791→   791→* Medical referral.
   792→   792→* Other arrangements.
   793→   793→Follow-up is optional.
   794→   794→Discharge statuses:

   838→   838→Package deduction rules
   839→   839→Deduct a session only when a qualifying linked therapy session is completed.
   840→   840→Do not deduct sessions for:
   841→   841→* Cancelled appointments.
   842→   842→* No-shows.

   851→   851→* Manual adjustment.
   852→   852→* Expiry.

  1190→  1190→25. Important Business Rules
  1191→  1191→The following rules are mandatory.
  1192→  1192→1. One patient can have multiple treatment episodes.
  1193→  1193→2. Each treatment episode maintains its own clinical records.
  1194→  1194→3. Appointments and therapy sessions are separate entities.
  1195→  1195→4. A completed appointment does not automatically mean a completed therapy session.
  1196→  1196→5. Only completed therapy sessions count towards progress review thresholds.
  1197→  1197→6. Cancelled appointments and no-shows must not count.
  1198→  1198→7. Attended appointments without treatment must not count.
  1199→  1199→8. Progress reviews default to every 15 completed sessions.
  1200→  1200→9. The review counter resets after each completed formal review.
  1201→  1201→10. Early progress reviews must be supported.
  1202→  1202→11. Treatment plans must have version history.
  1203→  1203→12. Historical diagnoses must be retained when diagnoses change.
  1204→  1204→13. Completed clinical records must not be silently overwritten.
  1205→  1205→14. Discharge is therapist-led.
  1206→  1206→15. Discharge closes a treatment episode, not the patient.
  1207→  1207→16. Outstanding payments must not block clinical discharge.
  1208→  1208→17. Package sessions are deducted only after qualifying completed therapy sessions.
  1209→  1209→18. Package deductions must be reversible through auditable transactions.
  1210→  1210→19. Insurance rates must be configurable by provider.
  1211→  1211→20. Historical invoices must preserve the rates originally applied.
  1212→  1212→21. Therapist remuneration rules must be configurable.
  1213→  1213→22. Reports must distinguish unique patients from total completed sessions.
  1214→  1214→23. Every protected backend operation must validate the user's permissions and clinic access.
  1215→  1215→24. Financial transactions must be protected against duplicate processing.
  1216→  1216→25. Clinical and financial history must remain auditable.

  1218→  1218→26. Clinical Workflow
  1219→  1219→Implement the following end-to-end workflow.
  1220→  1220→Patient Registration ↓ Appointment Booking ↓ Patient Attendance ↓ Initial Clinical Assessment ↓ Diagnosis ↓ Treatment Plan Creation ↓ Treatment Plan Activation ↓ Therapy Sessions ↓ Progress Review after every 15 completed sessions ↓ Clinical Decision ↓ Continue / Modify / Refer / Discharge ↓ Discharge Summary ↓ Close Treatment Episode

  1222→  1222→* A patient can be placed on hold.
  1223→  1223→* A therapist can initiate an early progress review.
  1224→  1224→* A diagnosis can be provisional.
  1225→  1225→* A treatment plan can be revised.
  1226→  1226→* A patient can be referred for further investigation.
  1227→  1227→* A patient can request to stop treatment.
  1228→  1228→* A patient can discontinue treatment.
  1229→  1229→* A patient can return later for a new treatment episode.
